# ACR - User Guidelines

**ACR** is the **Access Control Registration** application. It **enrols** a new
user by learning their **face** and **voice**, so that **MAC** can recognise
them later.

## What it is for
ACR answers *"register this person"*. Each run captures one person's face and a
short voice sample and stores them under the name they type. It is the companion
to **MAC** (which recognises enrolled people).

## Before you start
- A **webcam** and **microphone**, available (not held by another app).
- Good, even **lighting** on the face.

## How to use it
1. Launch **AcrApp**. The Speaking Avatar appears.
2. Press **Register**.
3. The avatar says *"Welcome to the CAV Access Control Registration Service.
   Please type your name."* - **type your name** in the box.
4. The avatar says *"Look at the camera."* - look straight at the camera.
5. The avatar says *"Please speak a short sentence so I can learn your voice."* -
   say a short sentence.
6. ACR stores your face and voice under your name and confirms the registration.
7. You (or the next person) can press **Register** again to enrol another user.

## Notes & troubleshooting
- **Registration fails / face not captured:** improve lighting, face the camera
  squarely, ensure no other app is using the camera.
- Each name should be **unique**; registering the same person under several
  names will make later recognition ambiguous.
- After enrolling, use **MAC** to verify the person is recognised.
